# Account and Card Payment Fee Dispute and Reversal Policy

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This policy defines the authority limits, eligibility criteria, and operational workflow for evaluating and granting fee reversals, credits, or waivers on customer deposit, credit card, and debit card accounts. The objective is to balance customer satisfaction and fair treatment with institutional risk controls.

## 2. Customer Identification and Verification
The agent must establish customer identity via Level 2 verification before disclosing account fee breakdowns:
1. Verify customer legal name and account number.
2. Confirm two knowledge factors (e.g., recent deposit amount and mailing zip code), or complete SMS OTP authentication.

## 3. Policy Rules and Courtesy Reversal Limits
1. Annual Reversal Limits: Tier 1 specialists possess discretionary authority to reverse up to two (2) qualifying fees per customer account per rolling 12-month period, up to a maximum cumulative value of $100.00.
2. Eligible Fees:
   - Monthly Account Maintenance Fees: Eligible if customer established required direct deposit or minimum balance within 30 days of assessment.
   - Card Payment Processing & Foreign Transaction Fees: Eligible for courtesy credit if the customer was unaware of international merchant routing.
   - Out-of-Network ATM Surcharge Fees: Maximum $15.00 waiver per calendar quarter upon request.
   - Late Payment Fees: Eligible for single courtesy waiver if customer has made on-time payments for the preceding 6 consecutive cycles.
3. Ineligible Fees: Court order processing fees, legal attachment fees, wire recall investigation fees, and certified check issuance fees are strictly non-refundable.

## 4. Standard Operational Next-Best-Action Procedure
When an authenticated customer disputes an assessed fee or requests a courtesy waiver:
1. Step 1: Open the Account Ledger in the Core Banking Portal and locate the specific disputed fee entry.
2. Step 2: Check the "12-Month Courtesy Fee Concession History" counter to confirm the customer has not exceeded the 2-waiver annual cap.
3. Step 3: Determine if the fee was assessed due to bank system latency or customer oversight. If bank error, classify as "System Adjustment" (does not count against customer courtesy cap).
4. Step 4: If eligible under courtesy rules, navigate to the "Fee Management" screen, select "Request Concession", and choose reason code "CUST-COURTESY-SATISFACTION".
5. Step 5: Submit the transaction. Inform the customer that the credit will reflect on their ledger within 1 to 2 business hours and explain how to avoid recurring fees going forward.

## 5. Escalations and Tier 2 Review
1. If the fee amount exceeds the Tier 1 discretionary threshold of $100.00, submit a "Supervisor Concession Approval Ticket" with documented customer hardship rationale.
2. If the customer requests waiver of fees assessed older than 90 calendar days, route the request to the Fee Exceptions Committee.
